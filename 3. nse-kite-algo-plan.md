# NSE intraday options bot — build plan (v1.2.5)

Status: design only. No code, no live orders, no “proven winning” claim.
Market: NSE cash + NFO, via your own Zerodha Kite Connect app.
You deploy to EC2 and manage keys, rules, data files, and tokens from the private UI.

This is an engineering plan, not investment advice and not a promise of profit. No indicator set wins every day. A 1:2 reward-to-risk system only needs to be right about 40% of the time **after costs** to break even, and option spreads, theta, and slippage are large enough to erase a weak signal. The edge, if there is one, comes from **not trading** most days.

## 2. What “good” looks like

A trading day has exactly one of these outcomes, all of them logged:

1. **Skip** — index sideways, no sector/stock passed anti-chase, no confirm before 14:30, expiry-day block, data unhealthy, or you did not arm the session.
2. **One filled trade** — on the best confirming stock option (or Nifty if `phase: nifty_only`), then flat by target, stop, stock-VWAP structure invalidation, or the 15:15 clock.
3. **Armed but not filled / Liquidity reject** — signal confirmed, limit order not filled within timeout and cancelled (or option spread/risk failed). Does **not** burn the day's trade; returns to `WATCHING`. A **fill** consumes the day's trade.

Broker positions are the source of truth, not the bot’s memory. After every restart the bot reads positions and open orders and resumes management. It never assumes it is flat because a local variable says so.

## 4. Market direction, sector filter & stock screener

Do market direction on the **index**, then filter **sector indices**, then shortlist **F&O stocks**. A stock rallying against a weak market and weak sector is far more likely to fail mid-day.

### 4.1 Step 1 — Index Bias (Market Regime)

- 09:15–09:30: record only. Build the opening range on near-month Nifty futures. No orders.
- From 09:30, recompute on every **closed** 5-minute candle, and once immediately at 09:30 using the completed 15-minute opening range.
- Opening range = Nifty futures high and low of 09:15:00–09:29:59, built off exchange timestamps.

Each line is −2, −1, 0, +1, or +2. Sum them:

| # | Input | Bullish (+) | Bearish (−) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nifty futures vs previous close | Above, and gap is not a tiny wick | Below |
| 2 | Nifty futures vs session VWAP | At least 0.15% above | At least 0.15% below |
| 3 | Opening range candle (futures) | Close in top 30% of 15m range, body ≥ 40% of range | Close in bottom 30% |
| 4 | 5m trend (futures) | Close > EMA 20 and EMA 9 > EMA 21 | Mirror |
| 5 | Breadth (Nifty 50 equities) | ≥ 60% above their own session VWAP | ≤ 40% |
| 6 | Bank Nifty futures agreement | Also above its VWAP and OR mid | Also below both |

Score:
- **≥ +6 and Bank Nifty not bearish → BULLISH → look for CE setups**
- **≤ −6 and Bank Nifty not bullish → BEARISH → look for PE setups**
- **Anything else → SIDEWAYS → no trade (do not scan/buy stock options)**

Sideways is the common case. If Nifty says up and Bank Nifty says down, force sideways. That one filter saves more money than any extra oscillator.

### 4.2 India VIX & Dead-Range Vetoes

- If India VIX is up ≥ 8% vs previous close **and** Nifty futures is still inside the opening range, veto the day (`VIX_UNSTABLE`).
- If Nifty futures opening range is `< 0.25 ×` the 15-minute ATR(14) (computed on spot `NIFTY 50` 30-day history to avoid futures rollover gaps), veto the day (`DEAD_RANGE`).

### 4.3 Step 2 — Sector Index Filter (`sector_fno_stock`)

Because Kite sector spot indices carry `volume = 0`, rank the 10 tracked NSE sectors (`NIFTY BANK`, `NIFTY IT`, `NIFTY AUTO`, `NIFTY PHARMA`, `NIFTY METAL`, `NIFTY FMCG`, `NIFTY ENERGY`, `NIFTY FIN SERVICE`, `NIFTY PSU BANK`, `NIFTY REALTY`) by **relative strength vs `NIFTY 50` spot**:

$$\text{sector\_rs} = (\text{sector\_chg\_pct} - \text{nifty\_chg\_pct}) + (\text{sector\_intraday\_pct} - \text{nifty\_intraday\_pct})$$

- **BULLISH day:** keep sectors where `chg_pct > 0`, `intraday_pct > 0`, and `sector_rs > 0`; take the top `2`.
- **BEARISH day:** keep sectors where `chg_pct < 0`, `intraday_pct < 0`, and `sector_rs < 0`; take the bottom `2`.

### 4.4 Step 3 — Top-20 F&O Pool → Anti-Chase → Best Stock Shortlist

1. Universe = all **F&O-eligible NSE stocks** mapped in `DATA_DIR/sector_fno_map.yaml` and verified against today's `NFO` options dump. (Non-F&O stocks are never scanned.)
2. Rank all F&O stocks by `% change` (top 20 gainers on `BULLISH`, top 20 losers on `BEARISH`). This **Top-20 pool** is logged and shown in the UI.
3. Filter for the **best actionable shortlist**:
   - Belongs to one of the **top 2 winning sectors** (`require_winning_sector: true`).
   - **Anti-chase gate:** `0.5% ≤ abs(pct_chg) ≤ 4.0%` (drops flat names and already-exhausted spikes).
   - Sort survivors by relative strength vs Nifty (`intraday_pct - nifty_intraday_pct`) and pick the top **`shortlist_size` (`5`, or `1` if you want strictly the single #1 stock)**.
4. At `09:30` (and whenever a new name enters the shortlist while `WATCHING`), warm up `1m` and `5m` bars (`prev_day 09:15 → now`) via Kite historical API **only for those shortlisted stocks** (~3–4 seconds total).

### 4.5 Warmup & What We Deliberately Do Not Use

Preload at `09:05`: `1m` and `5m` candles from `prev_day 09:15 → now` for near-month Nifty & Bank Nifty futures (backfilling any missed morning bars on late login/restart), plus `15m` spot `NIFTY 50` (~30 days) for ATR(14). VIX `prev_close` comes from tick `ohlc.close` with REST `quote` fallback.

Do not use:
- Raw top 20 gainers as an automatic buy button (without sector, anti-chase, or `orb_vwap` confirm).
- A standalone 1m RSI on Nifty before 09:30.
- News headline scrapers in the live loop.

## 5. Instrument, expiry, strike

### 5.1 Universe & Lot Size

- Default phase (`sector_fno_stock`): **NSE F&O stock monthly options** on the shortlisted stocks (`nifty_only` remains available via `universe.phase: nifty_only` in YAML).
- Lot size and tick size are **read from the daily NFO instrument dump**. Never hardcode lot sizes.

### 5.2 Expiry & Physical Settlement Guard

- Stock options and Nifty monthly options expire on the **last Tuesday of the month** (previous trading day if holiday).
- `min_dte_days: 7`: select the soonest monthly expiry strictly `>= today + 7 calendar days`. During expiry week (`< 7` days to expiry), the bot avoids the physically settled expiring month and checks next month (which only passes if spread & OI gates succeed).
- `skip_nifty_expiry_day: true` also blocks Nifty weekly expiry Tuesdays.
- From **7 Dec 2026**, NSE extends regular equity CAS (`15:30–15:40`) and non-expiring index F&O (`15:45`), while expiring index derivatives stop at `15:30`. Re-check session clocks and Zerodha MIS auto square-off before that date.

### 5.3 Strike: ATM

- ATM = strike closest to the **chosen stock's NSE cash `last` price** at the moment of `CONFIRM` (tie → lower strike).
- `BULLISH` → `CE`. `BEARISH` → `PE`.

Why ATM instead of OTM or deep ITM:
- OTM suffers from small delta and large % bid-ask spreads, turning a 30% stop into noise.
- Deep ITM ties up excessive capital per lot with wider spreads. ATM balances delta and order-book liquidity.

### 5.4 Liquidity & Rupee Risk Gate (Checked at Confirmation)

Reject that candidate stock's option if any fail:

- Bid or ask missing, or `bid <= 0`
- `(ask - bid) / mid > 1.5%`
- `oi < max(50000, 100 * lot_size)`
- Top-of-book `bid_qty < trade_qty` or `ask_qty < trade_qty` (when depth qty is present)
- Premium mid `< ₹40`
- Rupee risk of 1 lot at `30%` stop `> max_risk_inr_per_trade`

If stock #1 fails the gate, log the rejection and keep watching remaining shortlisted stocks (do not chase OTM strikes to force a fill).

**Important stock-option lot math:** Many NSE F&O stock contracts have notional values of ₹5–12+ lakh. A 2.5% monthly ATM premium is ₹12,500–₹30,000, making a 30% SL **₹3,750–₹9,000 per lot**. With `max_risk_inr_per_trade: 5000`, expensive-lot stocks will fail `RISK_CAP` and the bot falls back to the next shortlisted stock (or skips the day). Set `max_risk_inr_per_trade` intentionally to match your real capital.

## 6. Indicator sets

There is no published set that is “proven” for buying NSE intraday options. What works in practice is trend location (OR + VWAP) plus closed higher-timeframe candle confirmation, rather than piling on redundant oscillators.

Use **one** live set (`orb_vwap`) until it has at least 20 completed paper trades. `vwap_pullback` stays implemented but disabled (`active: [orb_vwap]`).

### Set A — `orb_vwap` (runs on the shortlisted stock's **NSE cash equity** candles)

**Regime (all must be true before watching 1m triggers):**
- Index Bias is `BULLISH` (for CE) or `BEARISH` (for PE), not vetoed.
- Time inside `09:30–14:30`.
- Stock is on the correct side of its own session VWAP, and `5m EMA 9 > EMA 21` (mirror for PE).

**1-minute trigger (arm on the stock, no order yet):**
- Closed `1m` stock bar closes `> stock OR high` (09:15–09:29 cash high) and `> stock VWAP`.
- `1m volume > 1.2 × average volume of previous 20 1m bars`.
- `1m RSI(14)` between `55` and `75` (mirror `25–45` for bearish). Below 55 is weak; above 75 is climax/exhaustion—do not chase.

**5-minute confirm (within next 2 closed 5m bars — only this may order):**
- Stock `5m` bar closes `> stock OR high`, `> stock VWAP`, green (`close > open`), `5m RSI(14)` between `52` and `72` (mirror `28–48`), `5m EMA 9 > EMA 21`.
- If a `5m` bar closes back across VWAP first, drop the arm (`confirm_failed`).
- First shortlisted stock to confirm wins and triggers option chain + liquidity + risk check. If that stock's option fails liquidity/risk, drop its confirm and keep watching remaining shortlisted stocks; otherwise cancel other arms and enter `ENTRY_WORKING`.

**Entry, Stop, Target & Structure Kill:**
- Decision time = timestamp when the `5m` candle is known closed (`5m` closes are processed before `1m` closes on simultaneous boundaries).
- Option LIMIT buy at **ask** (same in paper and live), 3s timeout; wait for terminal cancel if unfilled (`not_filled` returns to `WATCHING`). Fill price (not signal price) sets `R`.
- `R = 0.30 × fill premium`. Protective **`SL` (stop-limit)** sell (`trigger = fill - R`, limit `3%` below trigger; **never SL-M**). Target LIMIT sell at `fill + 2R`.
- **Structure kill:** checked on each closed `1m` **stock** bar—if stock closes back across its own VWAP against the position, exit immediately (`structure_exit`).
- Whichever comes first: premium stop, premium target, structure exit, `15:15` flatten.

### Set B — `vwap_pullback` (disabled until Set A has 20 paper trades)

For trends that already broke OR and pull back to value:
- Regime aligned + prior 5m bar closed outside OR.
- 1m trigger: low touches VWAP or `5m EMA 9`, closes back above both, and 1m RSI crosses up through `50` (mirror for bearish).
- 5m confirm: current 5m bar closes green above VWAP (`within_bars = 1`). Never allow two simultaneous positions.

### Set C — do not build

MACD + RSI + Stochastic + Supertrend + Bollinger on three timeframes. Correlated oscillators create noise, not edge.

### Entry sequence, one screen

```text
09:15  research, build OR (Nifty FUT + equities), no orders
09:30  score Index Bias
       SIDEWAYS or veto → watch and log, stay flat
       BULL or BEAR → rank top 2 sectors → Top-20 F&O → anti-chase shortlist (top 5)
       warmup 1m/5m for shortlisted stocks → watch 1m closes
1m close meets trigger on a shortlisted stock → state ARMED, log snapshot, no order
next 5m closes
       fail → state WATCHING, reason confirm_failed
       pass → check DTE>=7, ATM strike, liquidity, rupee risk, time < 14:30
              reject → drop this confirm, return WATCHING
              pass → LIMIT buy at ask
                     filled → place SL limit + target limit, state MANAGING
                     not filled (3s) → cancel (wait terminal), state WATCHING, reason not_filled
MANAGING
       tick through stop or target → exit, cancel sibling order
       1m stock close back through stock VWAP → structure exit
       15:15 → flatten whatever the P&L
       then state DONE for the day (terminal until tomorrow's rollover)
14:30  if still watching/armed/research → disarm, reason window_closed
```

## 7. Orders and the manage loop

Kite constraints that shape the code (verify before enabling live orders):

- Paid **Connect plan** active (includes WebSocket ticks and historical candles).
- Access token expires **06:00 IST next day**; manual daily login via redirect + 2FA required (no scripted login).
- Order rate limits ~10/sec, 400/min, 5,000/day; static IP whitelisting and SEBI self-use algo declarations apply.
- WebSocket allows up to 3 connections × 3,000 instruments (this strategy uses ~230 symbols).
- Historical candles ~3 requests/sec; 1m history up to 60 days per call.
- Tag orders (`tag`, max 20 chars) with `D{YYYYMMDD}{n:02d}` (counter persisted in SQLite across restarts).

Order design:

- Product `MIS`, variety `regular`, exchange `NFO`.
- Entry: LIMIT buy at **ask**, quantity = `lot_size × max_lots`. Check `orders()` before placing to prevent duplicate tags.
- Protective stop: **`SL` (stop-limit)** sell (`trigger = fill - R`, `price` 3% below trigger; **never SL-M**). Target: LIMIT sell at `fill + 2R`.
- Unfilled-stop watchdog: after `last <= stop_trigger`, if SL remains open > 2s, prefers `modify_order` in-place toward bid; if cancel/replace is used, cancels `target` first and waits for terminal `CANCELLED` before replacing.
- Sibling cancel: when either exit fills, cancel the other immediately.
- Partial fills: after 3s cancel remainder and wait for terminal broker status (`CANCELLED`/`COMPLETE`). Only return to `WATCHING` if final `filled_qty == 0`.
- 15:15 flatten: cancel stop/target, LIMIT sell at bid, step down toward bid twice over 5s intervals; never loop market orders.
- Reconciliation every 5 seconds (`positions()` + `orders()`): signed quantity check; local disagrees with broker logs `reconcile_mismatch` and follows broker; accidental negative net qty (`< 0`) covered with `BUY` limit; two mismatches → `HALTED`.
- Kill switch: cancel all working orders, flatten/cover any open position, halt new entries.
- Costs stored on every trade: brokerage (`₹20/order`), STT (`0.15%` sell option premium), NSE txn (`0.03553%`), SEBI, stamp duty, `18%` GST, plus slippage vs decision-time ask.
- Paper mode uses **ask** for entry fill and **bid** for exits, never forcing artificial 3% worst-case SL fills when a higher bid exists.

## 8. Logging, reasons, after the close

### 8.1 What gets logged to SQLite (`DATA_DIR/algo.db`)

Every state change, veto, bias computation, sector rank, shortlist, arm, confirm drop, liquidity/risk reject, order, fill, exit, reconcile mismatch, kill switch, and review save is written to `events` (with `ts`, `state_from`, `state_to`, `reason`, template-rendered `reason_text`, and full reproducible `snapshot_json`). Trades, orders, sessions, rules versions, app settings, engine heartbeat (`engine_status`), reviews, and operator commands are stored in dedicated SQLite tables.

### 8.2 After the market — manual review loop

1. By `15:40` the day card is ready (`sessions` + `trades` + `events` with MFE/MAE, exit reason, net P&L after costs, rules version).
2. Clicking **Draft a review** sends only the day JSON to the review model (requires `XAI_API_KEY` from `credentials.json`/env; never exposes secrets). The draft must pick 1–3 tags from the closed list:
   `bias_wrong`, `late_confirm`, `chop_after_entry`, `spread_too_wide`, `sl_too_tight`, `target_too_far`, `structure_exit_saved_me`, `structure_exit_too_early`, `expiry_noise`, `news_spike`, `no_signal_correct_skip`, `would_have_worked_if_chased`.
3. You edit and press **Save review**. Nothing is saved automatically.
4. Minimum **20 paper trades** (or 10 live trades) before editing YAML strategy thresholds, and a tag should appear on at least a third of losers to justify a parameter change.

### 8.3 UI Panels (6 pages on `127.0.0.1:8000` — zero Kite API calls from UI reads/polling)

1. **Today** —
   - **Live System Health Strip** (green/amber/red badges, updated from engine 2s heartbeat in SQLite):

     - **Ticker WS:** `LIVE (0.4s ago · 232 tokens · 4,820 ticks)` + live **Nifty Spot & Futures LTP** during market hours, Amber `Connected · Market Closed` outside market hours/holidays, or Red `DISCONNECTED / STALE`
     - **Candles & Warmup:** `Warmup: OK · Last 1m 09:34 · OR Ready`
     - **Mode:** `PAPER MODE (Simulated)` vs `LIVE MONEY MODE (Real Orders)`
   - Next-session mode switch (**Enable Live / Switch to Paper**, locked unless `LIVE_TRADING=true` in `/etc/algo/env`), state, armed status, Index Bias (6 lines), Winning Sectors, Top-20 F&O table, Active Shortlist, rupee risk cap/preview, Arm (works starting `08:45+` once token is verified) & 2-click Kill Switch.

3. **Trade** — **Cumulative P&L Summary Card (`Paper | Live | All`)** showing total trades, wins/losses, win rate %, gross, costs, net P&L, and avg R + multi-day trade history table, plus single-day trade breakdown (`underlying`, `sector`, option details, MFE/MAE).

## 9. Architecture & Engine Clock

Two continuous `systemd` daemons on one EC2 box (`algo-engine.service` and `algo-api.service`), able to start cleanly even before API keys/tokens are entered:

```text
you, each morning                  EC2 (private UI)
  Settings / Kite OAuth + 2FA ───► api process (FastAPI, HTTP Basic)
                                   writes token.json / credentials.json (0600)
                                   writes commands / app_settings / data files
                                          │
                                          ▼
                                   engine process (250ms asyncio loop)
                                     date rollover & 06:00 wipe
                                     2s heartbeat -> engine_status
                                     candle builder from WebSocket
                                     bias, screener, sets, risk, orders
                                     sqlite (algo.db WAL)
                                          │
                                          ▼
                                   Kite REST + KiteTicker
```

Stack: Python 3.12, `kiteconnect`, FastAPI, uvicorn, PyYAML, httpx, SQLite (WAL), `chrony` (`Asia/Kolkata`). No Redis, Kafka, Celery, or Docker required.

### Engine clock (IST)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| Every 250ms / 2s | Detect calendar date rollover (`DONE`/`BLOCKED` → `IDLE`, clear yesterday's memory); write 2s heartbeat + telemetry to `engine_status`; poll `ARM`, `KILL`, `RELOAD_TOKEN`, `RELOAD_CONFIG`, `VERIFY_TOKEN` even while `BLOCKED` or `DONE`. |
| 06:00 | Wipe expired token (`created_at < today 06:00 IST`) from memory and `token.json`; disconnect old WS. |
| 08:45 | Banner/alert if no token yet. You can login and click **Arm** anytime after token verifies (`08:45+`). |
| 09:00 | Latch scheduled rules (`trading_mode`), reload `DATA_DIR/` holidays/nifty50/sector map, download **NSE + NFO** instrument dumps (3 retries with 5s backoff). |
| 09:05 | Warmup `1m`/`5m` (`prev_day 09:15 → now`) for Nifty & BankNifty futures + `15m` spot Nifty 50 (ATR). |
| 09:10 | Health check: verify `profile()` + `margins_equity()` (into `engine_status`), instruments, chrony, `LIVE_TRADING` consistency, and open `KiteTicker` connection (`ws_connected`). |
| 09:15 | Subscribe Nifty/BankNifty FUT, spot Nifty, VIX, Nifty 50 equities, **10 sector indices**, and **all ~215 F&O equities** (`quote` mode). Populate `open_0915` (`ohlc.open` fallback if late). Tick freshness (`≤2s`) enforced from `09:15:05+`. |
| 09:30 | Score Index Bias. If directional, rank sectors, build Top-20, shortlist top 5 anti-chased stocks, warmup 1m/5m (`prev_day 09:15 → now`) for shortlist, run `orb_vwap`. |
| 14:30 | Disarm new entries (`window_closed`). Open trade continues managing. |
| 15:15 | Flatten working exits and open position via stepped limits. |
| 15:20 | Reconcile broker flatness. |
| 15:40 | Day card ready for Review (`DONE`, waits automatically for tomorrow's rollover). |

### State across crashes

On mid-day startup, engine checks today's `sessions` row, pulls `positions()` and `orders()` from Kite, resumes `MANAGING` (or reconciles mismatch) if a position exists, stays `DONE`/`HALTED` if terminal for today, or rebuilds candles (`prev_day..now`) and resumes `WATCHING` (clearing any old arm).

## 11. Safety limits (3-Lock Live Protection)

- **Default is Paper Mode.** Sending real orders requires **all 3 locks**:
  1. `LIVE_TRADING=true` in `/etc/algo/env` on the EC2 server (cannot be overridden from the web UI).
  2. `risk.trading_mode: live` in the scheduled rules YAML (promoted via UI/YAML **only for the next session**, never mid-session).
  3. Daily morning **Arm** click after manual 2FA login (plus passing `09:10` health check).
- First live month: `max_lots: 1` and a hard `max_risk_inr_per_trade` cap.
- No averaging down, no second trade, no overnight holds (`MIS` + `15:15` flatten), no short options.
- Halt on: WebSocket silent > 15s while managing (after REST quote fallback → flatten), incomplete warmup, stale instruments, clock skew > 2s during market ticks, double reconcile mismatch, or unexpected order rejection.
- UI is never on the public internet. Paper results include full costs and ask/bid fills.
